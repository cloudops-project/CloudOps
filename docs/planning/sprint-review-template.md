## Outcome and demonstration

List completed issues and show acceptance evidence. State what was planned but not completed and why. Never invent test, performance, security, or deployment results.

## Quality and security evidence

Record review/check/test results, tenant isolation implications, threat-model changes, accessibility checks, audit/log behavior, vulnerabilities, and approved exceptions.

## Metrics and feedback

Report measured values with environment/workload/denominator. Capture stakeholder feedback, decisions, and changed assumptions.

## Risks, blockers, and next work

Update the [risk register](risk-register.md), technical debt, dependencies, owner/reviewer, and proposed sprint objective. Update [project memory](project-memory.md) before handoff.
